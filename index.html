Case Study – Women Bags

I managed and optimized a messaging campaign for a women's fashion brand in Egypt, focusing on generating high-quality leads at a low cost.

Results

✔️ 59+ Qualified Messages

✔️ Cost per message: 7.5 EGP (~$0.15)

✔️ Total spent: 447 EGP (~$9.3)

✔️ High CTR & strong engagement

Strategy

  • • Tested multiple creatives to find winning product
  • • Focused on high-demand product (White Denim Bag)
  • • Scaled using duplication strategy instead of editing ads
  • • Optimized based on CTR & Cost per message

Key Insight

Strong ad performance with low cost per result. The main improvement opportunity was increasing conversion rate from messages to actual sales through better offers and sales handling.
