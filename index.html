Case Study — Women's Fashion Campaign

I managed and optimized a performance-driven Meta messaging campaign for a women's fashion brand in Egypt, focusing on creative testing, audience optimization, qualified message generation and improving campaign efficiency.

59+

7.5 EGP

Strong
CTR & Engagement

Strategy

  • Tested multiple creatives to identify the strongest-performing product angle
  • Focused budget on the highest-demand product and strongest creative
  • Used duplication to scale winning ad variations while preserving performance
  • Optimized campaigns based on CTR, engagement and Cost Per Message

Key Insight

Strong ad performance with low cost per result. The main improvement opportunity was increasing conversion rate from messages to actual sales through better offers and sales handling.

Performance Takeaway: The campaign demonstrated strong efficiency at the message-generation level, while highlighting the importance of connecting ad performance with the sales process to maximize final business results.
